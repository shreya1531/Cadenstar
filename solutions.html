BIM Modelling

Architectural, structural and MEP models built to specification.

Architectural BIM, structural BIM and MEP BIM models can be developed from design drawings, specifications, point-cloud data, PDFs or existing project information. The workflow typically starts with model setup and project standards, followed by discipline-wise modelling, coordination checks, information development and documentation. Models can be developed at the required level of detail and information for design, coordination, quantity workflows and construction documentation.

Typical scope

Architectural BIMStructural BIMMEP BIMAs-Built / Existing-Condition BIM

How it is developed

  1. Model setup & standards
  2. Discipline modelling
  3. Coordination review
  4. Documentation & data

Clash Detection

Identify and resolve conflicts before site execution begins.

Clash detection uses coordinated discipline models to identify hard clashes, clearance issues and interface conflicts before construction. Models are combined, rules and tolerances are applied, issues are reviewed by discipline, and coordinated reports are issued for resolution. The process helps teams address problems digitally instead of discovering them during fabrication or site execution.

Typical scope

Hard Clash DetectionClearance / Soft Clash ReviewModel FederationClash Reports & Issue Tracking

How it is developed

  1. Federated model
  2. Rule-based checks
  3. Issue classification
  4. Resolution tracking

Quantity Take-Off

Accurate BOQs and material planning from coordinated models.

Quantities can be extracted from coordinated BIM models and structured against project requirements. Elements are reviewed for correct classification, model data is checked, quantities are grouped into useful schedules and the resulting information can support BOQ preparation, procurement planning and cost review. Outputs depend on the model scope and the project's measurement requirements.

Typical scope

Model-Based Quantity Take-OffBOQ SupportMaterial SchedulesQuantity Validation

How it is developed

  1. Model audit
  2. Element classification
  3. Quantity extraction
  4. Schedule / BOQ output

Steel Detailing

Tekla models, shop drawings, GA drawings and fabrication support for steel delivery.

Steel detailing converts structural design intent into fabrication-ready information. The workflow can include 3D steel modelling, connection/detail development, general arrangement drawings, assembly and single-part drawings, material lists and revisions. Models and drawings are checked for constructability, consistency and fabrication requirements before issue.

Typical scope

3D Steel DetailingGA DrawingsAssembly & Single-Part DrawingsMaterial / Fabrication Schedules

How it is developed

  1. Model steelwork
  2. Develop connections
  3. Generate drawings
  4. Check & issue

Construction Coordination

All consultants working from one unified, clash-free model.

Construction coordination brings architectural, structural and MEP information together so project teams can work from a coordinated digital environment. The process includes model federation, interface review, clash resolution, coordination meetings and drawing/document updates. The aim is to make construction information easier to interpret and reduce avoidable site uncertainty.

Typical scope

Multi-Discipline CoordinationInterface ReviewClash ResolutionConstruction Documentation

How it is developed

  1. Federate models
  2. Review interfaces
  3. Resolve issues
  4. Update deliverables

Structural BIM

Coordinated structural models for engineering, detailing and construction workflows.

Structural BIM develops an information-rich model representing structural systems such as foundations, columns, beams, slabs, walls and framing. The model can be coordinated with architectural and MEP information and used as a basis for structural documentation, quantity workflows, detailing and downstream fabrication support.

Typical scope

Structural ModellingFoundation & Framing BIMStructural DocumentationFabrication Support

How it is developed

  2. Model structural elements
  3. Coordinate interfaces
  4. Produce documentation
