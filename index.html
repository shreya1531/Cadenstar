About CadenStar

Engineering information teams can trust.

CadenStar BIM Solutions provides BIM modeling services and BIM modeling for architects, engineers, fabricators, builders and contractors. Our building information modeling services combine accurate digital coordination with practical project delivery, supported by more than 10 years of industry experience.

Our work includes architectural BIM, structural coordination, BIM design, build information modelling, BIM building modeling, BIM and 3d modeling for metro structures, industrial facilities, commercial buildings, educational campuses, hospitality and complex steel projects. This approach supports building information management and reliable construction documentation, including revit 3d modeling and BIM information modeling for coordinated deliverables.
