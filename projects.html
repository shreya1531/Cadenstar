Project experience

Practical BIM support across different projects Globally

From commercial developments and healthcare facilities to institutional campuses, infrastructure and residential towers, CadenStar supports project teams with coordinated BIM and engineering information.

CadenStar supports healthcare, commercial, institutional, infrastructure, hospitality and residential projects across different scales and levels of complexity.

Our BIM and engineering deliverables are purpose-built around each project’s scope, level of detail, coordination requirements and delivery stage—providing the right information at the right time, rather than a one-size-fits-all solution.

From early-stage design development and multidisciplinary coordination to clash detection, constructability review, quantity take-offs, detailed modelling and construction documentation, our approach evolves with the project.

We collaborate with architects, engineers, consultants, builders, contractors and fabricators to create clear, coordinated and reliable project information that supports better decisions throughout the project lifecycle.

Whether supporting a single discipline or working as an extension of a multidisciplinary project team, CadenStar brings practical BIM expertise focused on coordination, accuracy and construction readiness.
