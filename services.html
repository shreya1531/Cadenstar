Core services

One engineering partner across the BIM workflow.

From early-stage bim services and bim 3d modeling to fabrication and construction documentation, CadenStar delivers structured project information for real-world execution. Our bim consulting services help teams plan, coordinate and document projects with greater clarity. This includes building information modeling, building information management and bim building information modeling for multidisciplinary teams. We also coordinate with model engineering consultants when specialist engineering inputs need to remain aligned with the central BIM model.
