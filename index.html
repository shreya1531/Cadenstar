About CadenStar

Engineering Information | Coordinated with Confidence

CadenStar is a globally connected BIM partner supporting project teams across the UK, US, Middle East and Australia.

We help architects, engineers, builders, contractors, consultants and fabricators by turning design information into coordinated, accurate and construction-ready digital deliverables.

With more than 10 years of industry experience, our team brings together architectural, structural, MEP and steel expertise to support projects from design development through coordination and construction documentation.

Our capabilities span architectural BIM, structural and MEP coordination, clash detection, quantity take-offs, BOQ, steel detailing, fabrication drawings and construction documentation. We work across commercial, industrial, educational, hospitality, infrastructure and complex steel projects.

At CadenStar, BIM is more than creating a 3D model. It is about connecting people, disciplines and information so project teams can identify issues earlier, make informed decisions and reduce costly rework during construction.
